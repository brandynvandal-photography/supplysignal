If this is an emergency, do not email. Overdose is a medical emergency — call 911. Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222. Never Use Alone is 1-800-484-3731 and will stay on the line with you.

Nightlight is a free, non-commercial reference for people who use drugs: county-level supply alerts, how to test a supply, what to do during an overdose, heat and dehydration, drug testing and supervision, and how to find help. It sells nothing and asks for nothing.

Getting help with the app

Email hello@nightlight.help. It is a small project, so a reply may take a few days.

Reporting something that is wrong

This is the most useful thing you can send. Every clinical claim is checked against a source before it goes in, and that still is not the same as being right. If something is inaccurate — especially anything that could get somebody hurt — please say so.

Helpful to include:

  • Which screen, and the wording you saw
  • What you believe is correct
  • A source, if you have one

Please do not include your own drug use, your location, or anyone else's, in a report. Email is not private, it is not encrypted in transit by default, and it is not covered by the protections built into the app. Describe the page, not yourself.

Common questions

There are no alerts for my county. That is normal and it is the common case. Most scans find nothing. An empty county means nothing has been publicly reported recently — not that the supply is safe. No test and no absence of alerts makes a supply safe.

Does it work offline? The app keeps working during a session once it has loaded. Emergency guidance is designed to be readable without a connection.

What do you do with my data? Nothing is collected. See the privacy page for why that is structural rather than a promise.

Is this medical advice? No. Nightlight reports what public sources have published. It does not verify, advise, or clear any drug as safe.

Accessibility

Nightlight is built to work with VoiceOver and other screen readers, at large text sizes, and in both light and dark themes. If something is unusable for you, that is a bug worth reporting — please email and say what you were trying to do.
