Privacy

Nightlight. Last updated 13 August 2026.

Nightlight is a free, non-commercial harm-reduction reference. It is operated by Brandyn Vandal and can be reached at hello@nightlight.help.

Nightlight collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party services of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, and nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

The rest of this page explains why that is structurally true rather than a promise, because the people this is built for can be arrested, evicted, fired, deported, or lose custody because of what they look up.

The lookup never touches the network

The obvious way to build this app would be to request data for your county when you select it. That would put a record of your county, timestamped against your IP address, in a server log. Instead every dataset is downloaded in full, identically for every user, and every search runs on your device against that local copy.

Selecting a county, searching a substance, or opening an interaction warning generates no network request at all. A server log, a network capture, and a subpoena to the host would all reveal the same thing: that someone opened the app.

What is stored on your device

No cookies. No local storage. Nothing is written to disk and nothing survives the session. Three values are held in per-session memory:

KeyHoldsWhy
sc.seenA timestamp of this visitSo the welcome card does not reappear on every screen
ss.themelight or darkOnly if you use the theme toggle
ss.langA language codeCurrently unused; the language comes from your device

None of these records a county, a substance, a page, or a search. There is deliberately no "last county viewed", because that single value is the one that would put a county somewhere another person could find it. All three are cleared when you leave, when the tab or app closes, and by the Quick Exit control.

Location

The "Near me" feature is optional and only ever runs when you tap it. If you allow it, your device's coordinates are compared against boundary data already on your device to work out which county you are in. No geocoding service is contacted, no coordinate is transmitted, and the coordinate is not stored. You can use the entire app without ever granting location access.

No third parties

The app loads no fonts, no analytics, no CDN, no map tiles, no embedded media and no error reporting. This is enforced by a Content-Security-Policy that permits the app's own origin and nothing else, so a future change cannot quietly reintroduce a tracker — the browser would block it.

Outbound links to health departments, crisis lines and research sources are ordinary links. Opening one takes you to that organisation, which has its own privacy practices. Every such link is marked no-referrer, so the site you arrive at is not told you came from Nightlight.

Quick Exit

A control in the header clears what is held, drops the app from the back history, and leaves for a neutral site. Two things it cannot do, stated plainly rather than implied away: entries already written to your browser or device history before you used it, and your browser's own cache, are outside the app's reach. Private browsing helps.

Children

Nightlight is not directed to children and is rated for adults. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Nightlight reports what public sources have published. It does not verify, advise, or clear any drug as safe, and it is not medical advice. Overdose is a medical emergency — call 911.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, there is no stored data for a future change to apply retroactively.
